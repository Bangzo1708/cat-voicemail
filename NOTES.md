# Cat Voicemail — candidate greetings (round 1)

Brief: one voicemail greeting as if recorded by a cat, max 120 characters.
Judge weighs, in order: (1) clearly a cat's voicemail + lands a laugh,
(2) joke specific to cats, (3) the cat has a voice/attitude of its own,
(4) works spoken aloud, punch arrives quickly, (5) fresh, surprising wording.
Avoided cliches: bare "meow" with no punchline, generic
"leave a message after the beep".

## Candidates

### V1 — 114 chars (CHOSEN)
> Chairman Meow here, napping in the box your bed came in. Leave treats, not messages — the vacuum never calls back.

### V2 — 112 chars
> Hi, it's Miso. I knocked your vase off the table on purpose, and I'd do it again. The human is just my roommate.

### V3 — 119 chars
> This is Biscuit. The human is asleep, so I'm in charge now. State your name and your snack offering, or I'm hanging up.

## Why V1 wins

Selection criteria (explicit): most on-brief, most original, most memorable
at first glance.

- **Most on-brief:** it IS the voicemail, in the cat's own voice from the
  first two words ("Chairman Meow here") — a named cat with a pompous title,
  not an owner describing a cat. Two cat-specific jokes, not one: sleeping in
  the box the bed came in, and the vacuum as the cat's nemesis.
- **Most original:** "Leave treats, not messages" twists the voicemail
  cliche instead of repeating it, and "the vacuum never calls back" is a
  punchline no generic greeting uses. V2's "I'd do it again" is a worn meme
  line, and its vase joke overlaps the previous draft in this directory.
  V3's "hanging up" is a human phone action, less cat-specific, and its
  punch ("or I'm hanging up") is flatter than the vacuum line.
- **Most memorable at first glance:** the name "Chairman Meow" lands
  instantly, the box joke is visual, and the final clause is a clean,
  quotable punch that reads well aloud in one breath.

## Final entry (exact text, 114 characters)

Chairman Meow here, napping in the box your bed came in. Leave treats, not messages — the vacuum never calls back.
